Bhumitra Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Bhumitra ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Bhumitra does not collect, store, or share any personal data from users.

We only provide access to publicly available land records and map data from official government sources.

How We Use Information

Since we do not collect personal data, we do not use, store, or process any personal information.

Third-Party Data Sources

Bhumitra displays publicly available data from official government portals, including:

  • https://bhulekh.ori.nic.in
  • https://bhunakshaodisha.nic.in

We are not affiliated with any government authority.

Data Security

We do not store user data on our servers. All data accessed is publicly available and retrieved securely.

Children's Privacy

Bhumitra does not knowingly collect any personal data from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
